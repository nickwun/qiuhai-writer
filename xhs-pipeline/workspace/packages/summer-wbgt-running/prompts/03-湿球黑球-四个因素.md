# 03-湿球黑球-四个因素

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Mindmap layout with four branches.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Sketch-notes style, warm cream background, macaron color bubbles, hand-drawn curved lines, simple weather icons.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 热压力看 4 件事
- 气温
- 湿度
- 太阳直晒
- 风
- 不是一个数字说了算

## Visual Concept
Female cartoon runner in the center. Four branches around her: thermometer, humidity cloud, sun, wind lines. Curved arrows connect them to a small central label 热压力. Keep text large and readable.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
