# 01-夏慢秋快-封面指南

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic photography, no external logos.
- Strong cover impact, sparse layout, clear safe zones.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Use a hand-drawn/cartoon infographic style with soft Xiaohongshu-friendly colors: warm cream paper, macaron blue, mint, lavender, peach, gentle coral accents. Keep clean, cute, fresh, and easy to scan. All text should feel hand-drawn.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 夏天跑得慢
- 秋天可能突然变快
- 慢不是退步，可能是在适应

## Visual Concept
Split scene: left half hot summer running track with strong sun and sweat, right half cool autumn track with falling leaves. Mixed male and female cartoon runners move from hot side to cool side. Sports watch heart-rate line changes from red unstable line to calm green line. Large title in upper half.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
