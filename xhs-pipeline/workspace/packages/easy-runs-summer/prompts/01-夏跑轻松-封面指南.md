# 01-夏跑轻松-封面指南

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4, 1242x1660 preferred.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic photography, no external logos.
- Strong cover impact, sparse layout, clear safe zones.

## Style
Sketch-notes style with warm cream paper background, macaron blue, mint, lavender, peach blocks, coral red accents. Slight hand-drawn wobble, paper grain, doodle arrows, stars, sweat drops, heart-rate line.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 夏天轻松跑
- 别跑成隐形强度课
- 真正该守住的是体感和恢复

## Visual Concept
Hot summer running track. Mixed male and female cartoon runners jogging slowly, looking at a sports watch. Behind them: sun, sweat drops, heart-rate wave, and a speech bubble with "轻松跑？". Main title large and eye-catching in upper half. Keep bottom-right clear for watermark.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
