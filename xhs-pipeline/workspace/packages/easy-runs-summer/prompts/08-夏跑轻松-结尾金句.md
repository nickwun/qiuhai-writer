# 08-夏跑轻松-结尾金句

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4, Chinese hand-drawn text.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Sparse ending quote card.

## Style
Sketch-notes, warm cream paper, soft sunset peach and mint accents, hand-drawn heart-rate line, small stars and footprints.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 跑慢一点
- 不是退让，是训练的一部分
- 守住体感和恢复，才能稳定跑完整个夏天
- 你夏天轻松跑会慢多少？

## Visual Concept
Mixed male and female cartoon runners jog side by side at an easy pace on a shaded evening track. Calm faces, relaxed posture. Large central quote. Interaction line at the bottom but above bottom safe zone. Leave bottom-right for watermark.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
