# 04-夏慢秋快-热适应

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Flow layout with four nodes.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Sketch-notes with warm cream paper, macaron blue and mint nodes, gentle coral highlights, doodle arrows, sweat and sleep icons.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 热适应不是硬扛
- 更早出汗
- 散热更快
- 心率更稳
- 恢复更可控

## Visual Concept
Male cartoon runner jogging easily in tree shade, not under harsh direct sun. Four circular nodes around him: sweat drop, wind cooling, stable heart-rate line, sleep moon. The mood should feel controlled and safe.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
