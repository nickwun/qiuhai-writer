# 08-夏跑中暑-结尾金句

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Sparse ending quote card.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, mint and peach accents, shaded path, water bottle, route sign, gentle but strong closing mood.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 停下来不是退缩
- 是对长期跑下去的尊重
- 别把危险信号当成意志力问题
- 你夏天跑步会提前停吗？

## Visual Concept
Mixed male and female cartoon runners stop under shade, rest, drink water, and check route sign. Large central quote. Calm, warm, safe, not dramatic.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
