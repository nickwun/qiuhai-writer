# 01-夏跑抽筋-封面指南

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic photography, no external logos.
- Strong cover impact, sparse layout, clear safe zones.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Use a hand-drawn/cartoon infographic style with warm cream paper, macaron blue, mint, lavender, peach, gentle coral accents. Clean, cute, fresh, educational. All text should feel hand-drawn.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 夏天跑步抽筋
- 真的只是缺盐吗？
- 别把原因想太简单

## Visual Concept
Hot summer running track. Mixed male and female cartoon runners pause and look at a calf cramp. Around them: salt tablet, electrolyte bottle, sweat drops, muscle fatigue icon forming a big question mark. Main title large and eye-catching.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
