# 08-夏跑抽筋-结尾金句

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Sparse ending quote card.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, mint and peach accents, shaded track, training review icons, calm reflective mood.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 身体不是缺一粒盐
- 是在提醒你，已经超过当前能稳住的范围
- 别只找单一答案
- 你最常在哪个阶段抽筋？

## Visual Concept
Mixed male and female cartoon runners sit in shade reviewing training. Around them are four small icons: pace, fueling, strength, weather. Large central quote. Calm and memorable.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
