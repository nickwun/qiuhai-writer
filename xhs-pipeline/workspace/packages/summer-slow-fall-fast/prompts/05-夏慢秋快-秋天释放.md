# 05-夏慢秋快-秋天释放

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Balanced layout with metaphor image.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream background, soft autumn peach and mint, hand-drawn leaves, backpack, light upward curve, clean composition.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 秋天像卸掉负重
- 热负荷下降
- 散热压力变小
- 有氧积累还在
- 体感突然变轻

## Visual Concept
Female cartoon runner removes a small backpack labeled 高温 and 湿度, then runs toward a cool autumn track with falling leaves. Show a light upward feeling curve. The card should feel relieving and hopeful.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
