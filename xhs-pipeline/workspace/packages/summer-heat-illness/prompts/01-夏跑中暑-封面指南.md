# 01-夏跑中暑-封面指南

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic photography, no external logos.
- Strong cover impact, sparse layout, clear safe zones.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Use a hand-drawn/cartoon infographic style with warm cream paper, macaron blue, mint, lavender, peach, gentle coral warning accents. Clean, cute, fresh, safety-oriented, not scary. All text should feel hand-drawn.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 夏天跑步
- 最怕不是累
- 是分不清热衰竭和中暑
- 这些信号别硬扛

## Visual Concept
Hot summer running track. Mixed male and female cartoon runners pause and look at a warning sign. Background has sun, sweat drops, heart-rate line, and a coral exclamation mark. Strong title in upper half. Avoid frightening medical imagery.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
