# 07-夏慢秋快-四个信号

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Checklist layout with four readable items.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, macaron sticky notes, small icons, soft check marks, hand-drawn training log and pen.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 跑对的 4 个信号
- 训练没有中断
- 轻松跑后能恢复
- 热天心率更稳定
- 天气一凉，体感变轻

## Visual Concept
Female cartoon runner holds a training log. Four colored checklist sticky notes with icons: calendar, sleep moon, heart-rate line, autumn leaf. Keep text large and readable.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
