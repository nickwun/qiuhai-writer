# 01-湿球黑球-封面指南

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic photography, no external logos.
- Strong cover impact, sparse layout, clear safe zones.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Use a hand-drawn/cartoon infographic style with warm cream paper, macaron blue, mint, lavender, peach, gentle coral accents. Keep clean, cute, fresh, and easy to scan. All text should feel hand-drawn.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 夏天跑步
- 别只看温度
- 真正要看热压力
- 湿球黑球温度 WBGT

## Visual Concept
Hot summer running track. Mixed male and female cartoon runners look at a weather app. A small thermometer is next to a larger "heat pressure dashboard" with sun, humidity cloud, wind, sweat drops. Main title large in upper half.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
