# 07-夏跑抽筋-预防清单

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Checklist layout with five items.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Sketch-notes style, warm cream background, macaron checklist cards, icons for pace, electrolyte, strength, training volume, logbook.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 减少抽筋看 5 件事
- 夏天配速别照搬
- 长距离先试补给
- 力量训练补上
- 跑量强度慢慢加
- 记录自己的抽筋模式

## Visual Concept
Male cartoon runner checks a training log. Five checklist cards with icons: pace watch, electrolyte bottle, dumbbell, training volume curve, logbook. Practical and save-worthy.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
