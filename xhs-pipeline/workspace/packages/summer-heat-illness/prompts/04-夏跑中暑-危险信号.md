# 04-夏跑中暑-危险信号

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Checklist layout, urgent but calm.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, coral red emergency card accents, macaron blocks, phone and cooling icons. Keep scene cartoon and non-graphic.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 这些信号要立刻求助
- 意识混乱
- 说话含糊
- 晕倒或抽搐
- 走路明显不稳
- 身体异常发热

## Visual Concept
Male cartoon runner sits in shade while a running friend holds a phone to seek help. Use emergency phone icon, cooling towel icon, and checklist. Avoid realistic hospital or injury imagery.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
