# 08-夏慢秋快-结尾金句

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Sparse ending quote card.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, soft autumn peach and mint accents, small footprints, falling leaves, gentle heart-rate line. Clean and emotional but not dramatic.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 夏天的慢
- 不是白费，是暂时没显示在配速上
- 可恢复地坚持，秋天才有机会兑现
- 你秋天会突然变快吗？

## Visual Concept
Mixed male and female cartoon runners jog side by side on an autumn evening track. Along their footprints, summer sweat drops gradually turn into autumn leaves. Large central quote with warm hopeful feeling.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
