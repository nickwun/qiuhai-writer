# 02-夏慢秋快-不是退步

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Comparison layout, moderate density.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper background, macaron sticky notes, coral warning accents, hand-drawn arrows and sports-watch icons.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 先别急着说退步
- 表面：配速慢了
- 表面：心率高了
- 真相：环境成本变高了

## Visual Concept
Male cartoon runner looks at sports watch with a worried face. Two red sticky notes say 配速慢了 and 心率高了. On the other side, sun, humidity cloud, sweat drops show environment pressure. Use arrows to reveal the real cause.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
