# 02-夏跑中暑-疲劳对比

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Comparison layout, moderate density.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper background, macaron pastel blocks, coral warning accents, hand-drawn split line, simple icons.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 累 ≠ 热衰竭
- 普通疲劳：停下会缓过来
- 热衰竭：头晕、恶心、发虚
- 关键：别边观察边继续跑

## Visual Concept
Male cartoon runner in two panels. Left: normal tired runner resting and recovering with green check. Right: dizzy sweating runner with warning sticker. Clear visual contrast without scary details.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
