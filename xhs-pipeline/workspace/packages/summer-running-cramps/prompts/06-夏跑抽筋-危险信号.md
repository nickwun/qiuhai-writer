# 06-夏跑抽筋-危险信号

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Checklist layout, calm warning tone.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, macaron sticky notes, coral warning icons, shaded rest area, non-scary safety style.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 别只当普通抽筋
- 持续不缓解
- 反复发作
- 头晕恶心
- 走路不稳
- 跑后深色尿或明显虚弱

## Visual Concept
Female cartoon runner sits in shade checking her body condition. Five warning sticky notes around her, with small icons. Clear and sober, not frightening.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
