# 06-夏跑中暑-处理四步

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Flow layout with four steps.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream paper, macaron flow nodes, hand-drawn arrows, cooling towel, water bottle, tree shade icons. Friendly and safety-focused.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 不对劲时先做 4 步
- 1 停止跑步
- 2 转到阴凉处
- 3 尽快降温
- 4 清醒时小口补水
- 意识异常，优先急救

## Visual Concept
Mixed male and female cartoon runners helping each other safely: stop running, move to shade, use wet towel for cooling, sip water. Final red reminder note: 意识异常，优先急救. No scary imagery.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
