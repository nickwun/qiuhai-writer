# 07-夏跑中暑-必须求助

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Checklist layout, clear emergency boundary.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Sketch-notes style, warm cream paper, coral warning checklist, phone icon, shaded rest area, soft but firm tone.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 必须求助的情况
- 意识不清
- 晕倒或快晕倒
- 抽搐
- 休息降温仍不缓解
- 拨打 120 或当地急救电话

## Visual Concept
Male cartoon runner resting in shade while a friend checks on him and holds a phone. A clear emergency checklist beside them. Keep image calm and non-graphic.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
