# 04-湿球黑球-同温不同感

Create a Xiaohongshu style infographic.

## Image Specifications
- Portrait 3:4 Xiaohongshu image card.
- Chinese hand-drawn text only.
- Hand-drawn/cartoon infographic, no realistic elements.
- Comparison split-screen layout.

## Xiaohongshu Visual Taste
Warm cream background, left panel coral warning tone, right panel mint relaxing tone, simple cartoon runners, strong contrast.

## Text Content
Use only this text:
- 同样 30°C
- 湿度高、没风、暴晒
- 湿度低、有风、有树荫
- 跑感完全不同

## Visual Concept
Left panel: runner sweating under hot sun with no wind and humid cloud. Right panel: runner relaxed under tree shade with wind lines. Put "30°C" large in center connecting the two panels. Mixed male and female cartoon runners.

## Watermark
Include a subtle watermark "秋海" in the bottom-right corner. Small, legible, low-contrast, elegant, not distracting.
